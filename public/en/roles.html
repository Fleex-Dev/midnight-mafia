Roles

The host may include the Detective and Doctor. Every setup still revolves around deduction, persuasion, and deception.

How to playGame rulesRolesPrivacy

Mafia

Choose one Citizen-team player to eliminate each night. Mafia teammates can see each other's current target in real time and must agree on the same player.

Citizen

You have no night action. Listen for contradictions and use the day vote to find the Mafia.

Detective · Optional

Investigate one other player each night. Only your screen reveals whether that player is Mafia.

Doctor · Optional

Protect one living player each night. If the Mafia target that player, the attack fails. You may protect yourself.

Default setup by player count

There is always at least one Mafia member, with approximately one Mafia per four players. The Detective is included by default, and the Doctor turns on automatically when the fifth player joins.

  • 4 players: 1 Mafia · 1 Detective · 2 Citizens
  • 5 players: 1 Mafia · 1 Detective · 1 Doctor · 2 Citizens
  • 8 players: 2 Mafia · 1 Detective · 1 Doctor · 4 Citizens
  • 12 players: 3 Mafia · 1 Detective · 1 Doctor · 7 Citizens
The host can turn the Detective and Doctor on or off in the lobby. Disabled special roles become Citizens.

Strategy tips

  • Mafia should avoid appearing either completely silent or needlessly aggressive.
  • The Detective must decide whether to reveal information early or save it for a decisive moment.
  • The Doctor may protect the same player on consecutive nights.
  • Citizens should track voting patterns and changes in each player's story.
