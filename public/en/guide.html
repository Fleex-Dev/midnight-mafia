How to Play

How to play

No app or account is required. Start immediately from any internet-connected phone or computer.

How to playGame rulesRolesPrivacy

Host: Create a game

  1. Open Midnight Mafia and enter the nickname you want to use.
  2. Select “Create a game” to generate a four-character room code and invitation QR code.
  3. Show the QR code or share the invitation link with the people playing.
  4. When everyone has joined, choose whether to include the Detective and Doctor, then start.

Players: Join an invitation

  1. Scan the QR code with your camera or open the invitation link you received.
  2. Enter a nickname and select “Join this room.”
  3. Confirm your name and the role setup in the lobby.
  4. Keep your role and every private action visible only on your own screen.
The room code identifies one game room. Share invitations only with people you intend to play with, not on public boards.

Using each screen

Lobby

View the player list, connection status, and role setup. A green dot means that player is currently connected.

Role reveal

Keep your screen private while revealing your role. The first day begins after every player marks themselves ready.

First day

There is no vote on the first day. Talk freely, then have every player tap ‘Ready for night’ to begin the first night.

Day and voting from day two

At night, choose a target and tap ‘Confirm choice’ to complete the action. The second day begins when all first-night actions are complete, and the Detective's result appears only on the Detective's screen. After discussing, each survivor taps ‘Ready to vote.’ Voting starts automatically when everyone is ready, and individual choices stay private until the result.

Game over

See the winning team and every role. The host can start another game with the same group.

If you cannot connect

  • Check your internet connection and refresh the page. A short interruption can be restored with the same player session within the default two-minute grace period.
  • Confirm the room code. Ambiguous characters such as I, O, 1, and 0 are not used.
  • You cannot join a game already in progress. Ask the host to create a new game.
  • If maintenance or a storage reset removed the room, create a new room and invitation.
