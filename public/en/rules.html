Game rules

Use your own phone to reveal your role, then find the hidden Mafia through discussion and deduction.

How to playGame rulesRolesPrivacy

Setup

  • Four to twelve players can join.
  • The host chooses whether to include the Detective and Doctor. The number of Mafia is set automatically.
  • After the game starts, each player privately reveals their role and marks themselves ready.

Turn order

  1. First day
    The first day begins after everyone reveals their role. There is no vote: discuss freely, then have every player tap ‘Ready for night’ to continue.
  2. First night
    The Mafia choose a target, the Detective investigates a player, and the Doctor protects someone. Each active role confirms their choice. The Detective sees the result privately as soon as the choice is confirmed. Citizens wait.
  3. Day two and voting
    Day two begins when every required night action is complete. The Detective's previous result remains visible only on the Detective's screen. After discussing, every survivor taps ‘Ready to vote.’ Each living player then privately votes for one other living player.
  4. Vote result and following rounds
    A separate result screen reveals a player and role only when that player received a majority of all living players' votes. A tie or a top vote below that majority eliminates no one. After every survivor confirms the result, the game either ends or proceeds to the next night and repeats the same sequence.

Vote threshold and role reveals

Elimination requires a strict majority: half the number of living players, rounded down, plus one. If the top vote is tied or falls below that threshold, no one is eliminated and there is no revote. Multiple Mafia can see each other's current target on the night screen. If their choices differ, only the Mafia must choose the same target again.

The role of a player eliminated at night or by vote is revealed to everyone. The player eliminated by vote is not included in the result confirmation count and no longer acts or votes.

Win conditions

Citizens win

The Citizen team—including the Detective and Doctor—wins when every Mafia member is eliminated.

Mafia wins

The Mafia wins when the number of living Mafia is equal to or greater than the remaining Citizen team.

The host only manages lobby settings and starts the game. After that, the server advances the game from the players’ choices.
