Browser Mafia Game

Play Mafia online with friends—no app or moderator needed

Midnight Mafia is a free browser-based social deduction game that runs role assignment, private night actions, day voting, and results for 4–12 people. Everyone plays from their own phone while the discussion happens face to face.

What you need

Bring 4–12 players and one internet-connected phone per person. There is no download, sign-up, shared account, or dedicated moderator.

Create a room, share its QR code or invitation link, and start once everyone has chosen a nickname.

Built for in-person play

  • Private role reveal and two-step confirm controls
  • Automatic day voting, night actions, and win detection
  • Optional Detective and Doctor roles
  • Live Mafia teammate selections visible only to the Mafia
  • Final role reveal, MVP, and player performance report

How a game works

  1. Create and join
    Share the room link or QR code and enter a nickname on each phone.
  2. Reveal roles
    Each player privately checks whether they are Mafia, Citizen, Detective, or Doctor.
  3. Discuss and act
    Talk face to face during the day, then choose and confirm private actions at night.
  4. Vote and review
    Vote from each phone. The game announces ties, insufficient majorities, exiles, and Mafia captures automatically.

Frequently asked questions

How many people can play?

Midnight Mafia supports 4–12 players and recommends the Mafia count automatically.

Does anyone have to sit out as moderator?

No. The website handles role assignment, private information, voting, night actions, results, and win conditions.

Do players need to install an app?

No. Open the invitation in a modern mobile browser. No account is required.

Can the Detective and Doctor be disabled?

Yes. The host can enable or disable each optional role in the lobby before the game starts.
