Game Rules

Mafia game rules and turn order

Use your own phone to reveal your role, then find the hidden Mafia through discussion and deduction.

How to playGame rulesRolesPrivacy

Setup

  • Four to twelve players can join.
  • The host chooses whether to include the Detective and Doctor. The number of Mafia is set automatically.
  • After the game starts, each player privately reveals their role and marks themselves ready.

Turn order

  1. First day
    The first day begins after everyone reveals their role. There is no vote: discuss freely, then have every player tap ‘Ready for night’ to continue.
  2. First night
    The Mafia choose a target, the Detective investigates a player, and the Doctor protects someone. Each active role confirms their choice. The Detective sees the result privately as soon as the choice is confirmed. Citizens wait. When every night action is complete, a separate Night Result screen reveals the night victim and role, or reports that no one disappeared. If the game continues, the next day begins after every survivor confirms the result. If the result decides the winner, the final report opens automatically after the reveal animation.
  3. Day two and voting
    Day two begins after the Night Result is confirmed. The Detective's previous result remains visible only on the Detective's screen. After discussing, every survivor taps ‘Ready to vote.’ Each living player then chooses one other living player and taps ‘Confirm vote’ to submit. The selection can be changed before confirmation.
  4. Vote result and following rounds
    A separate result screen reveals a player and role only when that player received a majority of all living players' votes. A tie or a top vote below that majority exiles no one. If the game continues, the next night begins after every survivor confirms the result. If the result decides the winner, the final report opens automatically after the reveal animation.

Vote threshold and role reveals

Exile requires a strict majority: half the number of living players, rounded down, plus one. If the top vote is tied or falls below that threshold, no one is exiled and there is no revote. Multiple Mafia can see each other's current target on the night screen. If their choices differ, only the Mafia must choose the same target again.

The role of a night victim or a player exiled by vote is revealed on its result screen. A player removed from the game by that result is not included in the confirmation count and no longer acts or votes.

Win conditions

Citizens win

The Citizen team—including the Detective and Doctor—wins when every Mafia member has been identified.

Mafia wins

The Mafia wins when the number of living Mafia is equal to or greater than the remaining Citizen team.

The host only manages lobby settings and starts the game. After that, the server advances the game from the players’ choices.
