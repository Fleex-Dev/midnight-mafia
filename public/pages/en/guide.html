How to Play

How to play Mafia without a moderator

No app or account is required. Start immediately from any internet-connected phone or computer.

Get started

Host

Create and invite

  1. Enter a nickname and select “Create a game.”
  2. Show the QR code or copy and share the invitation link.
  3. Once everyone joins, choose the optional roles and start.

Player

Join an invitation

  1. Scan the QR code or open the invitation link.
  2. Enter a nickname and select “Join this room.”
  3. Check your name and the role setup in the lobby.
A room code identifies one game room. Share the invitation only with the people playing.

Game flow

  1. Reveal roles
    Keep your screen private, reveal your role, and select “I'm ready.”
  2. First day
    Discuss without voting. The first night begins once everyone is ready.
  3. Night actions
    The Mafia choose an attack target, the Detective investigates, and the Doctor protects. Citizens wait.
  4. Night result
    Review the victim and role, or an all-safe result. Only the Detective sees the investigation.
  5. Day discussion and vote
    From day two, discuss and wait for everyone to become ready. Select a target, then confirm your vote.
  6. Day result
    A Mafia target is identified; any other role is exiled. A tie or insufficient majority removes no one.
  7. Next round or game over
    If the game continues, it advances after every survivor confirms the result. When a win condition is met, the final report opens automatically after the reveal and shows the winning team and every role.

Screen rules

  • Keep your role and night action hidden from other players.
  • Night actions and day votes use two steps: select, then confirm.
  • Only Mafia see the team selection status, and they must agree on the same attack target to finish the night.
  • If the screen sleeps or the connection drops briefly, the same browser reconnects automatically.

If you cannot connect

  • Check your internet connection and refresh the page. The game reconnects automatically when the phone screen turns back on. The same player session is retained for 30 minutes after a disconnect.
  • Confirm the room code. Ambiguous characters such as I, O, 1, and 0 are not used.
  • You cannot join a game already in progress. Ask the host to create a new game.
  • If maintenance or a storage reset removed the room, create a new room and invitation.

Frequently asked questions

How many people are needed to play Mafia?

Midnight Mafia supports 4–12 players and chooses the Mafia count automatically for the group size.

Does one player have to be the moderator?

No. The website handles role assignment, night actions, day voting, results, and win conditions automatically.

Is an app download or account required?

No. Open the invitation link in a modern mobile browser and enter a nickname to join.

Can we play without the Detective or Doctor?

Yes. The host can enable or disable either optional role in the lobby. Disabled special roles are replaced by Citizens.
