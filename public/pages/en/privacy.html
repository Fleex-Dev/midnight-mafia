Privacy

Privacy policy

Midnight Mafia processes only the minimum information required to run a game and does not require a user account.

Effective: October 13, 2026

How to playGame rulesRolesPrivacy

Information processed

  • Information you provide: the nickname used in a game.
  • Game state: room code, assigned role, alive status, ready status, night actions, and votes.
  • Information stored on your device: room code, temporary player token, and language preference.
  • Connection information: the hosting provider may process IP address, access time, and browser information in security and access logs.

Why it is used

Nickname and game state are used only to distinguish players, assign roles, synchronize the room across devices, resolve votes, and determine the winner.

Retention and deletion

  • Room state is stored temporarily in per-room Cloudflare Durable Objects storage. The service does not keep a separate database of completed game history.
  • A room is removed no later than eight hours after its last activity. It may disappear sooner following a service failure or an operator-initiated storage reset.
  • Your player session and language preference are stored in browser local storage. Leave the game or clear site data to remove them from your device.
  • Security access logs may be retained for a limited period under the hosting provider's operational policy.

Service providers and sharing

Cloudflare may process hosting, storage, and network connection information to deliver the service. Other players see only your nickname, alive status, and results required to conduct the game. Private choices are not disclosed before they are resolved.

The operator uses Google Search Console to review aggregated Google Search performance, including impressions, clicks, queries, pages, and countries, together with crawling and indexing status. A Google verification file is served from a public site path to confirm site ownership. The service does not add a separate Search Console visitor-tracking script or cookie.

No advertising or separate web visitor analytics service is currently enabled. Before either is added, this policy will identify the provider, cookies or similar technology, purpose, and retention period, and the service will provide any legally required consent controls.

Your choices

You may use a game nickname instead of your real name. Leaving the game removes that player from the current room and clears the local session; clearing browser site data also removes locally stored preferences. Server room data is removed after its inactivity retention period.

Safe use and changes

Do not use your real name or contact details as a nickname. Share invitation links only with the people playing. Direct privacy questions to the operator providing the service.

If features, hosting providers, or data practices change, this page and its effective date will be updated. Material changes will be announced in the service.
